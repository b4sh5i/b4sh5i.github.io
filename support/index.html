Support

A little help keeping your routine.

Updated September 16, 2026

When does the next reminder start?

The first reminder is scheduled one interval after you create or enable it. Once it is due, tap Done to start the next interval from the time you finish. With Count completions on, Pass skips this cycle without counting and starts the next interval from the time you tap it. A cycle does not advance until you respond.

Done, Pass and completion counts

Count completions is on by default and can be changed for each reminder. When on, Done adds one to today's completion count and Pass skips without counting. Both schedule the next alert after your chosen interval. Today's count appears on the reminder card and in iPhone and mirrored Watch alerts, including zero. When off, only Done is shown and new completions are not counted. Existing counts are kept and shown again if you turn counting back on. Counts reset at local midnight; there is no daily target or automatic stop.

How do follow-ups work?

Enable Follow-up reminders in the reminder editor. After the first alert, extra reminders are scheduled every 5 minutes, up to the count in Settings. The default is 5; choose 1–10. The first alert does not count. Changes apply to the next cycle or Remind again. When follow-ups end, choose Done, or Pass if Count completions is on. Remind again starts another set for the same unresolved cycle. Follow-up counts are separate from completion counts.

Why did a reminder stay quiet?

Check that the reminder is enabled and notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Interval. Check Sounds, Lock Screen and Banners, and whether Focus or Silent mode is affecting delivery. Sleep hours and unselected weekdays postpone reminders. Follow-ups stop after the configured count, and Today only ends at local midnight. If the app reports a scheduling problem, reopen it to retry.

Apple Watch notifications

Interval uses iPhone notification mirroring; it does not include a separate watch app. Delivery depends on your iPhone and Watch connection, lock state, notification settings and Focus. When the iPhone is unlocked, notifications usually appear on the iPhone. When it is locked or asleep, they can appear on an unlocked paired Watch. This is a regular notification, not a wake-up alarm that bypasses Silent or Focus.

Today only, sleep hours and travel

Today only expires at the next midnight in your device's local time zone. It is not marked Done automatically. Edit an expired reminder and turn Enabled on to start it again today. During sleep hours or unselected days, alerts wait until the next allowed time without using follow-up counts. If your device's time zone changes while Interval is not running, open the app once so existing alerts can be adjusted to the new local sleep hours and weekdays.

Three reminders

You can keep up to 3 reminders. Disabled and expired reminders also occupy a place. Delete one in its editor to add another. There are no in-app purchases.

Contact support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. Please leave private reminder names out of screenshots unless they are needed to explain the problem. Nothing is sent automatically from the app.
