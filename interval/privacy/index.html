Privacy Policy

Your reminders stay on your device.

Updated September 16, 2026

What the app stores

Interval stores reminder names, intervals, selected days, sleep hours, notification preferences, active reminder cycles and completion history locally on your device. You do not need an account.

What we collect

The app does not send your reminders or usage data to us. It has no advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs or cloud account service. We do not sell or share your reminder data.

Notifications

With your permission, Interval asks Apple's notification system to deliver local reminders. Reminder names may appear on your Lock Screen, in Notification Center and on a paired Apple Watch, depending on your settings. You can change notification permission and previews in iPhone Settings at any time.

Storage and deletion

Completion history is limited to the 1,000 most recent records. Records older than 90 days are removed the next time Interval runs. Deleting a reminder removes its settings, future notifications, active cycle and completion records. To remove all Interval data, choose Delete App in iPhone Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Interval. Offloading the app keeps its data. Device or iCloud backups may retain app data under your Apple backup settings; manage those backups through Apple.

Contact and website

If you email support, your email address and the message you choose to send are handled to answer your request. Do not include sensitive reminder details unless needed. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve your request, unless a longer period is required by law. You can request its deletion by replying. Opening a public support or privacy webpage shares standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser information, with the hosting provider. The provided pages do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes and questions

This policy applies to the current version of Interval. If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, use the contact options on the Support page.
